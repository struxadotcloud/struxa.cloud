## 2. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files saved on the user's device (e.g. computer, smartphone, tablet) when using the service. They enable, among other things, correct operation of the site, remembering preferences, and analysis of how the service is used.

## 3. Technologies we use

We may use:

- essential (technical) cookies,
- functional cookies,
- analytical cookies,
- browser local storage (localStorage) to save consent decisions.

For analytics we may use tools such as PostHog to measure performance and improve the service.

## 4. Purposes of cookies

We use cookies to:

- ensure the correct and secure operation of the service,
- remember user settings,
- analyse traffic and feature usage,
- improve service quality.

We do not use cookies to make automated decisions with legal effects on users.

## 5. Legal basis

Essential cookies are used on the basis of our legitimate interest in ensuring the operation and security of the service.

Optional cookies (e.g. analytical) are used on the basis of user consent.

## 6. Managing consent

On your first visit, you may accept or decline optional cookies. Your decision may be saved in browser storage and can be changed at any time via browser settings or by resetting your cookie preferences on the service (where available).

You may also:

- delete saved cookies,
- block cookies in your browser settings,
- restrict tracking technologies.

Restricting cookies may affect the correct operation of some service features.

## 7. Retention period

Cookies may be stored:

- for the session (until the browser is closed),
- for a defined period (persistent), depending on their function and configuration.

The exact duration may vary depending on the tool used.

## 8. Recipients of data
